# Phones, iPads, and foldables

The full layout detail behind the README's [Phone and iPad](../README.md#phone-and-ipad) summary. [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) has the rules the layouts follow.

## Touch

- Every control is a 44px target on a touch screen, iPads included; BB's title-bar buttons are 36px inside 44px targets, and resize handles are 12px wide. The gutter's **+** shows only with a mouse or trackpad; on touch, tap a line, then another for a range.

## iPad

- On an iPad, the layout is the desktop's at touch sizes, and the band stays while the on-screen keyboard is up. Resizing the window (Split View, Stage Manager, turning the iPad) hides a docked panel that no longer fits, and docks it again when there's room; nothing opens over the code by itself. The band fits its room as with a mouse. Where touch leaves little room, the bars make way: beside docked Comments with under 600px of code, the selection and step bars sit at the code's left. On a 13" iPad held upright with BB's thread list collapsed, docked Comments leaves the code that little room.

## Phone

- On a phone there's no band. A dock at the bottom holds **‹** (All reviews), the chapter you're on, **Comments** (how many aren't submitted, and a dot when something needs you), **Ask agent**, and **Full screen**. The chapter, Comments, and Ask agent switch one sheet above it between the chapters, Comments with Finish review, and the agent; a flick down, ✕, Esc, its tab, or a tap on the code above it closes the sheet. The chapters sheet holds Diff options and Review actions. The dock steps aside while you type or select lines, and toasts sit above it. Menus open as drawers from the bottom. On All reviews, ⌕ in BB's title bar opens the find box.
- A phone held sideways, or any window under 500px tall, has a 40px band and opens the chapters and panels only over the code, with the chapter intro collapsed. When the on-screen keyboard leaves a phone little room, the band, the notices, the file's sticky row, and Comments' Finish bar step aside for the box you type in, which comes into view with its buttons where they fit, and whole once the keyboard goes away.

## iPhone 14 Pro Max and iPhone Duo

- **iPhone 14 Pro Max and iPhone Duo**, held either way, and the Duo folded or unfolded. Folded, the Duo's outer screen gets the phone layout; unfolded, its inner screen gets the tablet layout, with the chapters and Comments beside the code where they fit: held sideways in Safari, Comments docks at 391px beside 560px of code, and in a home-screen app it opens over the code, right of the fold.
- Folding, unfolding, or turning either phone keeps the line you were reading, what's open (a docked panel becomes the sheet and back), half-typed text with its cursor, Find's text, the lines selected, and the focus, in BB too, which mounts the review anew as its layout switches at a 768px window.
- Held sideways beside BB's thread list (631px), the unfolded Duo still gets the tablet layout: the phone layout is for panes under 600px wide.
- Typing keeps the box above the on-screen keyboard, its buttons too, except where the Duo has room for the text box alone (folded and held sideways, and in BB folded or in Split View) until the keyboard goes away, when the whole box comes into view; where BB shrinks its pane above the keyboard, a phone stays a phone while you type and as the keyboard goes.
- In a home-screen app the review keeps clear of the notch, the Duo's side rail, and the home indicator, sideways too.
- On the Duo's inner screen the band's controls, the selection and step bars, menus, and Comments over the code keep off the fold (a band with under 600px of room in a phone's window ends with **Diff options** alone, which then holds the compare select and **Fold comments in code**); where Safari doesn't report the fold, its place is estimated.
